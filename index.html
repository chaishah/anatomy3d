Source & scope

Open Anatomy SPL Head & Neck

This version uses registered meshes from the Open Anatomy SPL Head & Neck Atlas. The source Slicer/MRML scene is converted once into compact GLB assets while preserving model transforms and anatomical registration.

3D Slicer is not required in the browser. It remains compatible with the source atlas for authoring and inspection, while the website uses optimized web-native GLB files for smoother interaction.

The atlas is an educational anatomical reference, not patient-specific imaging or medical advice. See ATTRIBUTION.md and the generated atlas manifest for source and modification details.
