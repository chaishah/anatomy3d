Attribution & scope

Anatomy3D Head & Vessels

This prototype uses the open-source Z-Anatomy skeletal and cardiovascular FBX models. The full upstream files are loaded at runtime, then filtered to the head inside your browser.

Written vessel summaries are educational reference notes based on standard anatomy sources, including NCBI Bookshelf/StatPearls. They are not medical advice.

See ATTRIBUTION.md in this repository for model licensing details.
