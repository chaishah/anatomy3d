Source & scope

BodyParts3D Detailed Head Atlas

This version uses detailed BodyParts3D / Anatomography v4.3 component meshes for head bones, teeth, arteries, veins, nerves, muscles, brain, eye and related structures.

The salivary study mode uses the real left/right submandibular and sublingual gland meshes available in BodyParts3D, together with nearby atlas structures. A separate parotid gland or major salivary duct mesh is not present in the current BodyParts3D v4.3 manifest used by this app.

The source geometry shares one atlas coordinate system. It is converted once to the Three.js coordinate basis and split into lazy-loaded web layers, preserving FJ and FMA identifiers for each selectable mesh.

BodyParts3D data is attributed to the Database Center for Life Science. See ATTRIBUTION.md and assets/bodyparts3d/manifest.json for source, license and modification details.

This application is an educational anatomy reference and is not diagnostic or patient-specific.
